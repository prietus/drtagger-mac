`tone.flac` is generated, not recorded: 8 s of a synthetic stereo chirp with
a little noise (44.1 kHz, 16-bit), encoded with `flac -8 -S 1s`, tagged with
`metaflac`, with 4 KiB of padding and a generated 800×800 JPEG as a PICTURE
block large enough (> 64 KiB) to be parsed as a reference. It carries no
third-party content and is covered by the repository's MIT license.
